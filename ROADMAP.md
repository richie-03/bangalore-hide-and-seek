# Roadmap

Ideas parked until after the first game. Nothing here is decided.

## Record during game one

- Time each hint was asked, and how much area the Seeker felt was left afterwards.
- Whether the endgame was reached, how many within-500-m asks it took, and how long the final walk was.
- Whether Relocate was used, when, and whether it felt too strong or too weak.
- Which challenges felt like chores and which felt like part of the game.
- Any rule that caused an argument or needed interpreting on the spot.
- Travel times between neighbourhoods at the time of day played.

## Balance levers (difficulty levels)

Each of these can move independently to favour one side. A difficulty preset is just a bundle of them.

- Head start length (30 to 45 min today).
- Hiding zone radius (400 m today).
- Number of hints the Seeker may use (6 of 8 today).
- Number of Relocates (1 today) and whether a Relocate refunds a hint to the Seeker.
- Endgame ask radius (500 m) and cadence (10 min).
- Game length (5 h) and endgame open time (3 h).

## Hint system, Jet Lag style

Group hints into categories with different costs or unlock rules, along the lines of:

- Matching: same neighbourhood, same nearest metro station, same nearest main road.
- Measuring: closer to A or B, distance bands from a point.
- Thermometer: has the Seeker got closer or further since the last ask.
- Radar: within N metres of the Seeker's pin (the current endgame ask, generalised).
- Photo: view down the road, nearest sign, nearest tallest building.
- Tentacles: which of a listed set of landmarks are within N metres.

Open questions: whether the Hider earns anything for answering (cards, time bonuses), and whether some categories should be locked until later in the game.

## App

- Visual redesign. The current board is functional and unstyled by intent.
- Separate Hider and Seeker interfaces: the Hider sees zone, Relocate, incoming asks; the Seeker sees challenges, hint menu, map.
- Shared state between phones so the chat is no longer the record of truth.
- A map view of the play area with the eliminated regions shaded.
- A rules editor or presets so a game can be configured before the start.
